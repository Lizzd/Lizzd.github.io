Biography

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at INSAIT (Institute for Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence and Technology, in partnership with ETH Zurich and EPFL) since July 2025, advised by Prof. Luc Van Gool and Prof. Jinjin Gu. My PhD is supported by the INSAIT PhD Fellowship.

My research focuses on multi-agent systems for visual media generation, editing, and understanding: agents that plan, call tools, and coordinate with each other to carry out complex creative tasks end to end, from turning a one-line idea into a narrated film (FrameWorkers) to turning a task description into an executable ComfyUI workflow (Knowledge-Centric Agents, ECCV 2026).

Before INSAIT, I completed both my Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Technical University of Munich (TUM), graduating with high distinction (top 5%). My Master's thesis, supervised by Prof. Daniel Cremers and Dr. Yan Xia, studied cross-modal place recognition (UniLoc). My Bachelor's thesis, supervised by Prof. Alexander W. Koch and Dr. Xingchen Dong, improved the resolution of hyperspectral imaging with structured illumination. During my graduate studies I spent one year as an exchange student in Computer Science at École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL), and worked on learning-based shared control for teleoperation with Dr. Youssef Michel.
